# Ohemaa Sustainable Housing — Design Direction

## Three stylistic approaches

### Theme Name: Coastal Civic Modernism
Very clean, quietly optimistic architecture with warm sand, deep teal, and editorial photography. The page feels trustworthy, public-minded, and rooted in place.

**Probability:** 0.07

### Theme Name: Earthbound Atelier
A tactile, craft-led direction using clay, paper, and natural textures with a more boutique real-estate feeling. The emphasis is on materiality and human hands.

**Probability:** 0.03

### Theme Name: Teal Infrastructure
A sharper, more graphic system with dark teal fields, modular statistics, and precise construction diagrams. The emphasis is on measurable impact and delivery confidence.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen approach: Coastal Civic Modernism

**Design Movement:** Contemporary African architectural editorial design, combining civic wayfinding with premium real-estate photography and restrained modernism.

**Core Principles:** The interface should feel grounded in Ghanaian place, make sustainability tangible rather than decorative, pair confident metrics with human warmth, and use whitespace as a sign of clarity and trust.

**Color Philosophy:** Deep teal is the signature color because it connects water, vegetation, and long-term resilience. Warm sand and clay keep the page from feeling corporate or cold, while off-white surfaces preserve readability and calm.

**Layout Paradigm:** An asymmetric editorial scroll: the hero pairs a strong text block with an architectural image, while later sections alternate dense information clusters with open breathing room. Content aligns to a consistent left rail rather than relying on centered cards everywhere.

**Signature Elements:** A slim clay-colored rule, oversized outlined numerals, and small uppercase civic labels create a recognizable visual rhythm. Image crops use organic rounded corners only where they soften the architecture.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Interactions should feel deliberate and reassuring. Buttons lift subtly, nav links reveal a clay underline, and the reserve flow opens as a focused dialog rather than sending visitors into a dead end.

**Animation:** Use short, 180–260ms ease-out transitions. Hero content enters with a slight upward drift, cards reveal in a staggered sequence, and hover states change transform and shadow only. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

**Typography System:** Use Fraunces for large headlines and metric numerals to create a human, editorial voice. Use DM Sans for navigation, body copy, labels, and controls. Headlines are tight and expressive; body copy is spacious with a 1.65 line-height.

**Brand Essence:** Ohemaa makes attainable Ghanaian homes from overlooked materials, for families who want dependable ownership without compromising the future. Personality: **grounded, inventive, generous**.

**Brand Voice:** Headlines are direct and hopeful; CTAs are specific and calm; microcopy explains rather than oversells. Example lines: “Build a better starting point.” “See how your home comes together.”

**Wordmark & Logo:** A compact interlocking roof-and-leaf mark sits beside a lowercase wordmark with generous tracking. The mark is bold enough to stand alone as a favicon and visual stamp.

**Signature Brand Color:** Ohemaa Teal — `#0B6B58`, a deep blue-green that feels both ecological and infrastructural.
